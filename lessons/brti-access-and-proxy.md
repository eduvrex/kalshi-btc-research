# BRTI has no free feed; we consume its inputs instead of scraping its output

BRTI publishes once per second, computed from constituent-exchange order books; CF Benchmarks' API requires a commercial license and their website is a throttled, session-limited renderer (stalls requiring refresh were observed; their terms page also blocks automated fetches). Scraping the site was considered and rejected as trading infrastructure: it adds render+relay latency on top of the 1 Hz snapshot, depends on an adversary-controlled surface, and the stalling IS the failure mode. Instead the proxy consumes the index's own inputs directly: 1 Hz mean of Coinbase, Kraken, Bitstamp, and Gemini book mids (four BRTI constituents, all free public websockets, upgraded from two on 2026-08-27). Calibration ground truth: settled 15-min markets expose official BRTI 60s averages every 15 minutes, so proxy bias and residuals are measured continuously without a license. Polymarket's RTDS relays Binance and Chainlink BTC free — cross-references, not BRTI.

Why it mattered: the proxy is computed from the same books BRTI is computed from, with no publication delay. Every mispricing measurement inherits the proxy's error band; the DQ report quantifies it before any signal built on it is trusted.
