## Caveats (read before believing any number above)

- Fills assume the next minute's opening quote had enough size for the position. We have no historical Kalshi depth — this is unverifiable until the live collector accumulates book data. Thin quotes make these fills optimistic.
- Decision granularity is 1 minute (candle data). The hypothesis is about second-scale dislocations; this backtest structurally cannot see most of them. A negative here does not kill the second-scale thesis; a positive here is encouraging but inflated by the caveat above.
- The spot composite (Coinbase+Kraken trades) is a proxy for BRTI inside the window. Strike and settlement values are official BRTI, so outcomes are exact, but intra-window x/z carry proxy noise.
- Fees are amortised over 100-contract orders. A literal 1-lot order pays the $0.01 per-order minimum ceil — at tail prices (the thesis regime) that is 1.6–14× the amortised figure, enough to flip thin edges negative at small size.
- Threshold sweep = multiple comparisons. Judge the pattern across thresholds, not the best row.
- t-stat assumes independent trades; overlapping volatility regimes violate this somewhat.
